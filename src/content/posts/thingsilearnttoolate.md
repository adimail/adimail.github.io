title:"Things I Learned Too Late..."
---

_Things I learnt too late as a person who have had responsibility for an outcome that depended on other people and limited resources._

1. Trust yourself and take the first step.
1. Create a team that stays aligned and committed.
1. Pursue problems that are worth solving.
1. Make incentives work in your favor.
1. Put your energy into a few important priorities.
1. Keep communication direct and easy to understand.
1. Remove unnecessary rules and red tape.
1. Focus on what gets accomplished.
1. Exceptional people can multiply a team’s impact.
1. Act quickly, learn, and adjust.
1. Accept constraints you cannot change.
1. Don’t let hesitation stop you from acting.
1. Growth can create unexpected effects.
1. Build things that become more valuable over time.
1. Learn from setbacks and keep moving.
1. Build alongside people you genuinely respect.
1. Search better problems.
